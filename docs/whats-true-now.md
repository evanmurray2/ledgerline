# What's true now

`docs/design.md` (Oct 31 design) and `docs/sync-spec.md` are kept as written. The system has moved on in the places below. Where a section there disagrees, the decision named wins (`docs/decisions.md`, `docs/decisions/`); each replaced section starts with a **Now** note. The Money rules, the budgeting model and the matching mechanics are unchanged.

One line per entry; add yours at the end of its list. This file merges with `merge=union` (`.gitattributes`, D-59): after a local merge, check for an entry listed twice.

## Design (`docs/design.md`)

- **Data:** the design said one D1 database. Now SQLite in Durable Objects: a `Directory` (users, sessions, routing) and one `BudgetStore` per budget (`docs/multi-user.md`). D-37, D-38.
- **Background work:** the design said Workflows and three crons. Now alarm jobs inside each store (sync, 4-hour backstop, daily scan at 5:00 AM and maintenance at 2:20 AM in the budget's time zone); one cron at 10:20 UTC wakes the Directory. D-37, D-38.
- **Who works out the budget:** the design said the Worker, on every request, with cached month summaries. Now the phone: it keeps an encrypted copy (`GET /api/sync`) and works out every screen with the same code; the server stores what was entered and imported, and keeps nothing derived. D-45, D-46, D-53; `docs/phone-budget.md`.
- **Sign-in:** the design said Cloudflare Access. Now passkeys in the Worker. D-27.
- **Raw Plaid pages and backups:** the design said R2 and a nightly encrypted export. Now raw pages in SQLite; no off-site backup; Cloudflare's 30-day point-in-time recovery, plus a full backup download from Settings. D-26, D-29, D-37.
- **Sync sizes:** the design said 500 per Plaid page, a Workflow step per page. Now 100 per page; 25 events per apply or processing step. D-6, D-38.
- **Environments and deploys:** the design said staging, then production after approval, with release, nightly and monitor workflows. Now no staging; every merge to `main` deploys after its `ci` passes; only `ci`, `deploy` and `security` workflows. D-30, D-36, D-49, D-51.
- **Database layer:** the design said Drizzle over D1. Now the app's own small interface over SQLite (`packages/db/src/sql.ts`). Nov 3 cleanup.
- **Tabs and their header:** the design said Budget, Activity, Accounts, Reports, each with its own title and Budget with its own month. Now Budget, Activity, Reports, Accounts; one `‹ November 2026 ›` bar in the same place on Budget, Activity and Reports with one month for the three, Activity by month (`docs/month-header.md`). D-58.
- **Progress tracking:** `docs/build-plan.md` lists open work only; work up to Nov 4, 2026 is in `docs/build-history.md`. D-59.
- **Rows before the budget start:** the design said they're used for payee learning and editable like any row. Now read-only (no category, note or Owed to you), never listed: not in Activity (searches too), registers, category sheets or Settings → Payees. Reports and net worth still add them up. D-61, D-62.
- **Categorization:** the design said an ordered rule list, then the payee's remembered category. Now no rules and no remembered category: Needs category suggests the category you've used most for the payee and you OK it (`docs/payees.md`). D-60.
- **Budget start, pending at link:** the design said a row pending at link is budgeted even if dated earlier. Now the row's date decides: rows dated before the start are never in a category; one not in the balance at link (pending then, or sent late within 14 days) moves the starting balance, and a link-day row comes out of it. A Starting balance sheet on each account shows the steps. D-67 (`docs/decisions/D-67-start-date-rule.md`).
- **Payee normalization:** the design said payees come from cleaned bank text (prefixes, store numbers, city stripped; title case) and look-alikes join. Now Plaid's values as sent: merchant id, merchant name and Plaid's name are the keys, compared only by case, outer spaces and apostrophes; a new payee's name follows Plaid only while it's new; existing transactions were filed again once (`docs/payee-names.md` 4–8). D-63 (`docs/decisions/D-63-payee-names-from-plaid.md`).
- **Closing an account:** the design had `account.closed_at` but nothing set it. Now Close account runs only at $0 (bank, pending, working balance and a card's payment category); it hides the account and archives a card's payment category; a new bank row or an entry opens it again. D-73 (`docs/decisions/D-73-close-account.md`).
- **Accounts and sign-up:** `docs/multi-user.md` 7.3 and 7.4 said invites made by an admin, a People screen and a reset link. Now open sign-up with a passkey, built and switched off (`SIGNUPS`), recovery codes, bank links for admins only (`docs/open-signup.md`). D-75 (`docs/decisions/D-75-open-signup.md`).
- **Recovery:** a point-in-time restore is still a runbook step, and now it can be undone: the Directory keeps the undo bookmark and `POST /api/settings/restore` takes `{ undo: true }`. D-77 (`docs/decisions/D-77-undo-restore.md`).
- **Multi-user:** what D-75 built stays, switched off; nothing new is built for other users until Plaid Production. D-78 (`docs/decisions/D-78-multi-user-frozen.md`).
- **Sign-up asks for no name:** `docs/open-signup.md` 3.1 said a Name field. Now sign-up takes a passkey and nothing else; no name or email is kept. D-85 (`docs/decisions/D-85-privacy-design-answers.md`), the first built step of `docs/privacy.md`.
- **Overview:** `docs/architecture.md` describes the system as built, on one page.
- **Net worth:** the design said the sum of every linked account balance. Now it counts pending, like the Cash and Credit cards totals on Accounts, so the three add up; Reports' current month and "in November" follow. D-76 (`docs/decisions/D-76-net-worth-counts-pending.md`).

## Sync spec (`docs/sync-spec.md`)

- **Storage and jobs:** SQLite in Durable Objects instead of D1, and alarm jobs in each budget's store instead of Workflows (D-37, D-38; `apps/worker/src/store/jobs.ts`). A failed step backs off and is tried up to 6 times.
- **Recalculation:** the phone works out budget lines, month snapshots and Ready to Assign from its own copy, with the same `computeBudget()`; the server keeps nothing derived and has no recompute (D-45, D-46). The guarantee in point 3 holds on the phone (`apps/worker/test/engine.test.ts`).
- **Limits:** D1's limits no longer apply; Durable Object rows read and written, requests and Worker requests do, and SQLite's CPU counts toward the 10 ms (D-40; `docs/measurements.md`).
- **Rules:** processing writes no rule results; it finds the payee, transfers and Waiting to match, and sets no category. D-60.
- **Matching mechanics, manual pending:** your entry pairs with a bank row only on exact evidence, else "Same purchase?" asks; it covers the bank's pending row (hidden, the entry counts) until the charge posts and takes the entry's key; batch order link, posted ↔ bank pending, posted ↔ entries, bank pending ↔ entries (`docs/payee-names.md` 9.1, 9.2). D-64 (`docs/decisions/D-64-entry-covers-bank-pending.md`).
- **Matching mechanics, posted ↔ bank pending without the link:** pairs only on exact evidence (same merchant by Plaid's merchant id or names, or the same amount on the same day), else "Same purchase?" asks; no text likeness or store numbers (`docs/payee-names.md` 9.3). D-65 (`docs/decisions/D-65-pending-match-without-text.md`), changing D-47 and D-50.
- **Matching mechanics, a posted row that took a bank pending row's key:** it was never compared with your entries, so a leftover entry counted twice with no question. Now it meets them by the same rule (exact evidence, else "Same purchase?"), in its batch and in the heal step, and the entry takes the pair. D-92 (`docs/decisions/D-92-posted-row-on-a-pending-key-meets-entries.md`), changing D-64 and D-72.
- **Bank difference:** a muted line on Accounts and the account page when starting balance plus posted rows differ from the bank balance, not a badge. D-66 (`docs/decisions/D-66-bank-drift-line.md`).
- **Pending in the bank balance:** pending the bank balance already holds counts once in the working balance; the bank difference is what pending can't explain, shown once it has lasted a day. D-68 (`docs/decisions/D-68-pending-in-bank.md`).
- **Overfunded, Set aside:** the design said a > N. Now only the extra assigned that is still unspent counts, min(a − N, available). D-69 (`docs/decisions/D-69-set-aside-overfunded.md`).
- **Starting balance on screen:** no note on Accounts; the account page notes a late row for 7 days, and its Starting balance row moves to the ⋯ menu once final with nothing new. A card's page has no Payment category row; Budget's card row shows it. D-70 (`docs/decisions/D-70-starting-balance-quieter.md`), changing D-67 item 5.
- **Add on a phone:** D-54 put every screen action in the header. Activity's Add is now a round button at the bottom right, above the tabs, on a phone only. D-71 (`docs/decisions/D-71-floating-add.md`).
- **Entry dated after the bank row:** D-64 let an entry pair with a bank row dated at most 1 day before it. Now up to 7 days, and the heal step after each sync also looks at recent posted rows an open entry could pair with. D-72 (`docs/decisions/D-72-entry-after-bank-row.md`).
- **Budget order and filters:** categories you pin show first (Pinned), saved with the budget so every device shows them; the Low filter is gone, Low shows only as the row's status word. D-74 (`docs/decisions/D-74-pinned-categories.md`).
- **Close and Cancel can be undone:** rule 11 had no way back. Now Reopen undoes either; a close undone in a later month stays in its own month, and the undo moves the amount back in the month of the undo. D-80 (`docs/decisions/D-80-reopen-owed.md`).
- **Removing an overspent category, and entries before the budget start:** both are refused for now; nothing in the design covered either. D-79 (`docs/decisions/D-79-refuse-overspent-remove-and-early-entries.md`).
- **CSV history import and the CSV/JSON exports:** removed; Settings → Backup and export keeps the full backup and the usage export, and rows imported before stay as history. D-81 (`docs/decisions/D-81-no-csv-import-or-data-exports.md`).
- **A split whose amount changes:** the design said the "absorbs remainder" line takes the difference. Now the lines stay, the gap counts as uncategorized, and Activity's Check these asks you to fix the split. D-82 (`docs/decisions/D-82-split-gap-you-fix.md`).
- **A transfer whose other side hasn't arrived:** "Which account got this money?" listed only accounts with a matching row. Now any other account can be picked; a pending entry is added there, paired, and the bank's row takes it over. D-83 (`docs/decisions/D-83-transfer-other-side-pending.md`).
- **CI and the deploy gate:** every merge ran all of `ci` again on `main` before deploying. Now a set of files that already passed as a pull request isn't tested twice, a docs-only change runs only `checks`, and a merge that changes only docs doesn't deploy. D-84 (`docs/decisions/D-84-ci-runs-what-changed.md`).
- **A deposit with no category:** it counted as uncategorized and waited in Needs category. Now money into checking or savings is in Ready to Assign until you choose a category; a credit on a card, and a deposit that looks like a transfer with no other side yet, still need one. D-85 (`docs/decisions/D-85-deposits-default-to-ready-to-assign.md`).
- **A transfer whose two sides arrive in different bank updates:** they paired by themselves only when they were filed in the same batch. Now a new row also pairs with an earlier row that has no partner, by the same rule (same amount, another account, dates at most 5 days apart, exactly one possible partner); a pair that was made stays; a row you sorted by hand is never paired (D-90).
- **The order bank updates are filed in:** two banks' sync jobs alternated step by step, so their rows were applied and filed interleaved. Now each bank still fetches side by side, but the updates are applied and filed one at a time, whole, in the order their fetches finished, for the whole budget (D-86 A2).
- **The time of what a bank update brings:** a row's arrival and removal, a vanished pending row, the bank balance time, the start of a bank difference, the day of a card's liability snapshot and the 44-day heal window each used the clock of whichever step ran, and "history changed" used a fixed time zone. Now all of them use the moment the update finished fetching, and "history changed" uses the budget's time zone (D-86 A1). D-67 item 3 and D-68 item 2 read that time.
- **An edit made while a bank update is being filed:** it was written at once, between two steps of the update. Now it waits until the update is filed (at most 10 seconds), so the budget is always the updates so far and then the edit (D-86 A4).
- **The time of an edit sent later:** everything it wrote, except which edit of one field is newer, used the moment it arrived at the server. Now a queued edit's rows carry the time the person made it, never later than the server's clock and at most 7 days back; sign-in and limits still read the server's clock (D-91).
- **Edits with no signal:** six kinds of edit worked offline (category, memo and month of a transaction, assign, target, pin, rename a payee). Now most edits do: adding and deleting an entry, owed items, matching, transfers, "Same purchase?", alerts, names and order of accounts, categories and groups. Archiving or removing a category, closing an account, joining payees, setup and anything with a bank still need a connection (`OFFLINE_EDITS` in `apps/web/src/copy/copy.ts`).
- **Migrations of a budget's store:** one list, which the server and every phone's copy both ran. Now two: budget migrations (`packages/db/budget-migrations`, run by both) and store migrations (`packages/db/store-migrations`, the server's `BudgetStore` only), so a server-only table never reaches a phone. The first store table holds the marks "sealed" and "frozen", which every old route, job and webhook obeys (`apps/worker/src/store/marks.ts`); no budget is marked yet.
- **Where the server counts a budget's banks:** from `connection`. Now from `bank_link`, a server-only table with a copy of each link and its encrypted token, kept in step with `connection` by triggers until the cutover (store migration 0002). The cursor, lock and fetch state are still in `connection`.
- **The time on a balance and a card snapshot:** the row's `updated_at` (a balance) and `created_at` (a card's daily snapshot) were the clock of the step that wrote them. Now they are the bank update's time, like the rest of what an update writes (D-86 A1), so a device that files the same update writes the same rows (`endOfUpdate` in `apps/worker/src/sync/process.ts`).
- **More times of a bank update:** a bank row's `updated_at`, a reopened account's and card category's `updated_at`, and the `created_at` of each bank log row were the clock of the apply step. Now they are the update's time too (D-86 A1), found when a device filed the same update from the inbox and its rows differed by a few milliseconds (`apps/worker/test/inbox.test.ts`).
- **Closing an account the bank no longer updates:** D-73 needed a bank balance of $0 and nothing pending. Now Close also runs at a working balance of $0 when every pending row is your own entry; the entries stay as final rows, the closed account counts as $0, and changing such an entry opens it again. D-93 (`docs/decisions/D-93-close-with-your-pending-entries.md`).
