# Personas

Guesses about who might use Ledgerline and what they do with it. They are not research: no one was asked. The
`personas` hunt (`docs/hunts.md`) has one agent play each type in the real app with a fake Plaid and report where
it was slow or confused. Change a type when real use (the owner's usage file, feedback) shows the guess was wrong.

Each agent records, per task: taps, wrong turns (a screen opened and left with nothing done), errors, time, and a
screenshot of the worst moment. It reads only what is on the screen: no code, no docs, no test ids.

## 1. Paycheck to paycheck

Paid every two weeks; rent takes most of the first check. Opens the app on payday and when a bill comes.

- Give the new paycheck a job: fill rent, then groceries, then what's left.
- A bill came in higher than planned: move money from another category to cover it.
- Check, in one glance, whether there's enough for groceries until the next payday.

## 2. Two people, one budget

Shares the budget with a partner (each on their own phone). Both spend from the same card.

- Categorize the partner's purchases they don't recognize.
- One of them paid for something the other owes back.
- Both edit the same category on the same day; see that the numbers agree.

## 3. Irregular income

Freelancer; money comes in uneven amounts at uneven times.

- Budget this month only with money already in hand.
- A large payment arrives: hold some for taxes and next month.
- See how many months of costs are already covered.

## 4. Once a month

Opens the app once a month and catches up on everything at once.

- Categorize 60+ new rows quickly, many from the same payees.
- Find out which categories went over last month and why.
- Fix last month's overspending before starting this month.

## 5. New to zero-based budgeting

Has never given every dollar a job. Comes from a spreadsheet or nothing.

- First run: link a bank (the fake), make categories, budget the first month.
- Understand, without help, what the number for money with no job yet means.
- Make a mistake (assign more than there is) and get back out of it.

## 6. Debt payoff

Two credit cards with balances, paying them down.

- Spend on a card and see that the payment money moves with it.
- Pay more than the minimum on one card.
- Track how fast the balances fall.
