When does your budget start?

Transactions from this month on go in your budget. Anything earlier is history: you can search it and see it in reports, but it never changes what you have to assign.

Your starting money is what's in your linked checking and savings today. What you owe on cards isn't subtracted: each card gets a payment category to fund instead. You'll assign it in step 3.
