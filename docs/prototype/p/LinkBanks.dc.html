Linking opens Plaid's secure window; Ledgerline never sees your bank password. We ask for 6 months of history, which can't be changed later. Older history can be imported from a CSV file in Settings.

Only bank accounts and cards are tracked. Investment and retirement accounts stay outside the app; money you send to them counts as Invested.
